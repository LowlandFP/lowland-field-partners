No online checkout

This website does not sell products, collect deposits or take card, bank or crypto payments. An email enquiry is not an order.

Paid research

A paid research brief starts only after written agreement on the question, deliverables, price, timing, acceptance criteria, contracting details and payment method. Those written terms control cancellation and refunds.

Before work starts

If no work has started and no non-refundable cost has been agreed in writing, either party may normally withdraw by email. Any exception must be stated clearly before acceptance.

After work starts

If work has started, any payment for work already completed and any refund are assessed under the written agreement and applicable law. A third party declining an introduction does not by itself create a guaranteed refund or commission.
