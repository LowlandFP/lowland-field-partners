Accessible by design

Accessibility statement

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Implemented basics

  • Semantic headings, navigation landmarks, labels and keyboard-reachable controls.
  • Visible focus states and clear button labels.
  • Responsive layouts and readable contrast on light and dark sections.
  • No decorative stock images or third-party embeds are required to understand the content.
  • Icons and emojis are decorative or accompanied by text; there are no essential image-only instructions.
  • Reduced-motion preferences are respected where animations are used.

Known limitations

This is a static multi-page site. Accessibility can vary by browser, device and assistive technology. Some sector pages use compact inline layouts and are being standardised progressively.

Report a barrier

Email lowlandfieldpartners@gmail.com with the page URL, device/browser and the barrier you encountered. Do not include passwords or sensitive files.
