Privacy & contact information

Lowland Field Partners (LFP) is an early-stage independent international B2B introduction-research initiative. Contact: lowlandfieldpartners@gmail.com. This notice explains how information submitted in business correspondence is handled. It is not a claim of formal company registration or an existing supplier mandate.

Email-based enquiries. The contact form on our homepage prepares a message in your own email application. It does not transmit your entries to an LFP website database or send them automatically. Your email application sends the message only if you choose to send it.

Information we may receive

If you email us, we may receive your name, work email address, organisation, country, business requirements and other information you include. Please avoid sending identity documents, bank details, CVs, personal candidate information or confidential technical files in an initial enquiry.

Purpose and basis of processing

We use incoming information to read and respond to enquiries, assess potential business requirements, check whether a relevant introduction may be possible and maintain correspondence records. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on steps requested before an agreement, legitimate interests in responding to relevant business communications, or consent where required. We do not assume that receiving an enquiry authorises us to share identifying or confidential information with another company.

Sharing and referrals

Before forwarding identifiable buyer, supplier or contact details to a third party, we seek appropriate permission and check the purpose of the proposed introduction. Initial market-fit research can be discussed in anonymised terms where appropriate. We do not sell contact lists or share candidate CVs as part of our services.

Email providers and website hosting

Business correspondence is handled through Google's Gmail service. This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical access information under its own applicable privacy terms. LFP does not intentionally deploy advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this website. The external services you use to send an email may process information under their own policies.

Retention and security

We keep business correspondence only while it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, legitimate follow-up, recordkeeping or applicable legal obligations, and review records when no longer needed. Email is not an appropriate channel for highly sensitive documents. No online communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to correct or delete information, object to further direct contact, withdraw consent where applicable, or request access to information we hold about you. We will consider requests subject to applicable law and any lawful retention requirements. Email lowlandfieldpartners@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. You may also have the right to complain to a competent data protection authority.

Commercial transparency

LFP may seek a referral or introduction commission from a supplier under separate written terms. No supplier is presented as an authorised partner without confirmation. Supplier contracts, quotations, product specifications, delivery, installation and after-sales responsibilities remain with the relevant supplier.

Last reviewed: 28 October 2026. This notice may be updated if the way we handle enquiries changes.
