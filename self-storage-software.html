Business software & operations research

Self-storage software and operational systems

Operators of self-storage facilities may need a combination of online unit booking, customer billing, access management and operational reporting. Lowland Field Partners researches possible supplier introductions based on a specific business requirement, without claiming to represent any software vendor.

What to specify before comparing suppliers

Facility and unit setup

Country, number of locations and units, unit sizes, occupancy, and whether the facility is new or already operating.

Customer acquisition

Website integration, live availability, online reservations, pricing rules and checkout requirements.

Billing and administration

Recurring invoices, local payment methods, taxes, arrears management and accounting exports.

Access and integrations

Gate and smart-lock compatibility, API requirements, identity verification and existing hardware.

Migration and data

Current platform, import/export requirements, data protection, hosting location and access controls.

Implementation and support

Required go-live date, staff training, support language, service hours and supplier responsibilities.

Buyer qualification checklist

  1. Identify the operating legal entity and facility country; provide a business website if available.
  2. Explain the operational problem and which functions are mandatory versus optional.
  3. State the number of sites/units, existing systems and integration constraints.
  4. Give an approximate budget and intended procurement or launch date, if known.
  5. Confirm whether the requirement is active and whether anonymised specifications may be discussed with potential suppliers.
