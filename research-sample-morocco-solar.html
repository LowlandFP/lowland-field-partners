GPM Services / GPM Holding

GPM's publicly accessible company description presents solar O&M services and expressly lists panel cleaning, electrical inspections, inverter maintenance and performance monitoring.

  • Evidence: services described on GPM's own website
  • Unknown: actual client sites, project scale, operational equipment, procurement priorities and willingness to evaluate external robots
  • Next step if authorised: verify service coverage and whether the company actively seeks equipment; do not presume interest

Documented market context, not an order

MASEN's public e-tendering portal lists the NOOR PV Multisites request for qualification for a contractor to handle EPC and operation & maintenance, reference RFQ/15/2026/MASEN, with a published deadline of 30 November 2026, 12:00 GMT. This demonstrates a published procurement activity in solar EPC/O&M; it does not establish a requirement to purchase cleaning robots or an opportunity for LFP to bid.

Method and limitations

Methods: review public organization descriptions and one official purchasing notice; record the URL for each source; distinguish company claims from independently verified capabilities; avoid extrapolating procurement or purchase intent. Sources checked for this example on 2 November 2026. Information may change and should be re-verified before a commercial decision.

  • Not a verified supplier audit, a tender submission or a commissioned study.
  • No due diligence on legal registration, certifications, deployment history, financial health or machine compatibility has been performed.
  • A paid assignment would define a specific market, candidate limit, source standard, delivery period and acceptance criteria in writing.
