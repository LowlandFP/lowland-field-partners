Scope

Lowland Field Partners (LFP) is an independent, early-stage B2B introduction and research initiative. It may help clarify a business requirement, research potential suppliers or buyers and coordinate an introduction. LFP is not automatically a distributor, agent, importer, exporter, broker of record, legal representative, logistics provider or technical adviser.

No guaranteed result

Information on this website, a public lead, a reply or an introduction is not proof of stock, authority, financing, an order, a contract or a commission. Each party must perform its own legal, quality, sanctions, import/export, financial and counterparty checks.

Written scope and fees

No fee is created by visiting the website or sending an enquiry. Paid research, referral fees or introduction commissions require a written agreement that states the parties, scope, fee or percentage, qualifying event, payment timing and any territory or protection period.

Supplier and buyer responsibility

Suppliers remain responsible for their products, certifications, technical specifications, quotations, contracts, delivery, installation, warranties and customer support. Buyers remain responsible for their purchasing decision, due diligence, payment and compliance obligations.

Acceptable use

Do not use the website to submit malware, impersonate another party, disclose confidential information without permission or request assistance with fraud, evasion, sanctions violations or unlawful activity.

Website information

We try to label uncertainty and remove unsupported claims. Sector pages describe areas we explore; they do not mean that LFP stocks, represents or endorses every product, company or technology mentioned.

This page is plain-language operational information and is not tailored legal advice. Before accepting paid work, confirm the correct contracting entity and applicable law.
