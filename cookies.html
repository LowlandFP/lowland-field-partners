Cookie and storage policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Current status

This website currently uses no optional analytics, marketing cookies, advertising pixels or third-party embedded media. We do not build an advertising profile from visits.

Local browser storage

A small local browser preference may record that you dismissed the privacy notice. It is stored on your device and is not sent to LFP as a visitor profile. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

Necessary technical processing

GitHub Pages and normal web delivery may process technical request data needed to serve and protect the website. This is not used by LFP for marketing profiling.

Future optional services

If optional analytics, advertising or embedded services are added later, they will remain disabled until a clear, freely chosen consent mechanism is provided. Refusing optional tracking will not block the public site.
