Independent B2B introduction research

Recruitment & business services: start with a real business requirement.

Lowland Field Partners explores potential introductions between organisations with genuine commercial needs and established service providers. Areas of research may include recruitment partners, employer-of-record (EOR) services, operational consulting and outsourced business processes.

Important scope limitation. LFP is an early-stage independent introduction-research initiative. We are not a recruitment agency, staffing supplier, employer of record, labour lender or immigration adviser. We do not employ or place workers, provide employment contracts, guarantee hiring outcomes or claim any existing provider mandate.

What we need to assess a business request

Organisation and location

Company, country, target market and an official business contact.

Service requirement

Recruitment service, EOR provider, operational consultancy or another clearly defined business service.

Scope and timeframe

Countries involved, indicative scale, procurement stage and expected decision window.

Provider requirements

Relevant licences or registrations, local legal compliance, service responsibilities and preferred commercial model.

How a potential introduction is evaluated

We first clarify whether the need is current and whether the business wishes to explore a third-party provider. We then research whether a suitable, independently verified provider has relevant country coverage, lawful operating permissions and a written referral or introduction process. No provider relationship, exclusivity or commission entitlement is assumed.

Before any identifiable contact or confidential requirement is shared, we seek the organisation's permission and the relevant provider's written lead-acceptance and commercial terms. All exploration is handled by email. The provider, not LFP, is responsible for proposals, screening, employment law, contracts, onboarding, payroll and service delivery.

Data protection and candidate information

Please do not send CVs, candidate names, personal telephone numbers, immigration documents, salary histories or other personal or sensitive employee information. We assess business requirements, not individual candidates. No personal data is forwarded to a provider without a lawful basis and appropriate permission.
